MonuAI

Landmark Recognition AI on Android

Project Overview

MonuAI is a landmark recognition AI assistant designed to help locals and tourists easily identify landmarks and instantly access relevant information about them, reducing the need for a physical tour guide and preventing missed opportunities to appreciate important sites. Built as a university project for a computer vision module, it focuses on solving a common tourism challenge: visitors often encounter iconic locations without clear, contextual information about their history, significance, or nearby points of interest. MonuAI was originally designed for emerging smart glasses, enabling a hands-free experience where users simply look at a landmark to receive on-the-spot insights, but it was later integrated into a mobile phone app to simulate a compact wearable device. The system is implemented using Flutter on Android, with the landmark recognition model running fully on-device, allowing for fast responses and offline-friendly usage while showcasing practical, real-world deployment of computer vision in an accessible application.

My Key Features

Model Distillation

Train data with larger model (YOLOv11m) and distill knowledge to smaller model (YOLOv11n) for on-device use

Live detection

Using phone camera to detect landmarks with bounding box, labels and information in real-time

Points/Reward System

Incentivizes user engagement through a points and rewards mechanism

Offline on-device AI

MonuAI runs the landmark recognition model fully on-device, enabling fast responses and offline-friendly usage.
